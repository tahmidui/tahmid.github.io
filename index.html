About

Social questions, explored with data

Doing social research is my way of giving back to the world.

I am a sociologist and Master of Science student in Data Science at Texas Tech University. I use quantitative research, survey data, and computational methods to understand how beliefs, social conditions, and culture shape well-being.

My MA thesis examined religion and subjective well-being using World Values Survey. I am now expanding my toolkit in Python, R, SQL, statistical modeling, data management, and machine learning while continuing to explore social questions through data.

Teaching is also central to my work. I have taught and mentored students in various settings and developed large-scale digital learning content in sociology.

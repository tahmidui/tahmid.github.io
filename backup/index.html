Areas of Interest

These are the things I love to do!

Sociology of Religion

I consider exploring the association between religions and well-being as my vocation. In my master’s thesis at Texas Tech, using the World Values Survey, I am analyzing the relationship between religiosity (beliefs and practices) and Subjective Well-Being (happiness and life satisfaction).

Mental Health

My MA thesis also analyzes the impact of religiosity (religious beliefs and practices) on well-being and determines whether beliefs or practices serve as stronger predictors. I am also working on another project that analyzes life satisfaction for people with disabilities.

Disability Studies

I am developing a research project with Dr. J. Dalton Stevens, Assistant Professor of Sociology at Texas Tech University, that uses the National Health Interview Survey (NHIS) to explore the association of religious attendance with life satisfaction across disability and life course timing.

Data Analysis

I often get my hands dirty with raw data; this makes me feel like a detective! At Texas Tech, I took courses on R programming and quantitative methods. In the spring of 2026, I am taking a course on content analysis, since I have plans to conduct qualitative projects in the future.

Teaching

As a Graduate Teaching Assistant at Texas Tech University, I help undergraduate students thrive by grading their assignments and providing them with personalized feedback. Before my graduate career, I taught a wide range of students, ranging from first graders to older people, which helped me to build strong interpersonal skills, manage large classroom settings, and understand students’ needs in different contexts.

Writing

Since 2015, I have been writing in Bengali newspapers and magazines on various topics ranging from satires to deeply rooted social problems. By writing on different platforms, I have learned how to initiate dialogue using audience-specific languages.
